# Knowledge Entry Guideline

## Topic structure

Use one lowercase, hyphenated folder per topic:

```text
knowledge/{topic}/
├── {topic}_info.md
└── references/
```

The info record is the readable, current synthesis. The `references/` folder
holds source files, links, excerpts, or other supporting material needed to
verify it. Create `references/` only when the topic has material to retain.

## Entry quality

State the topic, the relevant information, why it matters, and the source or
basis for claims that can change or need verification. Separate confirmed facts
from interpretation, proposals, and open questions. Date time-sensitive facts.

Write one focused record rather than accumulating unrelated notes. Update the
existing topic when new information belongs there; create a new topic only when
its subject is genuinely distinct.

## Safety and maintenance

Do not commit credentials, private personal information, or confidential source
material without explicit authorization. Remove obsolete claims or mark them as
superseded, retaining a short explanation when the prior view influenced later
decisions. Keep the root README master index in sync with topic additions,
renames, and removals.
