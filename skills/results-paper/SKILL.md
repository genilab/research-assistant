# Write Results Paper

## Scope

Use when the researcher says `Write results paper for {topic}` and identifies
the experiments and findings to report. It receives linked plans, result
catalogs, an intended contribution, audience, and scope; it produces an indexed
topic-local results-paper draft. Ask for clarification when the evidence set,
claim scope, or target paper form is unclear.
Primary agent role: writing-agent.

## Workflow

1. Read `../writing-refine/docs/scholarly-writing-standard.md`,
   `../experiment-plan/docs/experimentation-standard.md`, and the linked
   experiment plans and result catalogs.
2. Structure the paper as abstract, question and contribution, related work,
   method, results, interpretation, threats to validity, implications, and
   references, adapting only to an explicitly required venue format.
3. Link every empirical claim to a catalogued result, represent material null
   and conflicting outcomes, and distinguish reported results from inference.
4. Write `publications/results-paper-{slug}.md` and update its publication
   index and topic `INFO.md` entry.

## Constraints

- Do not infer missing methods, data, results, baselines, or significance.
- Do not recast an experiment plan as an outcome or suppress a material limit.

## Dependencies

- `experiment-plan`
- `result-catalog`
