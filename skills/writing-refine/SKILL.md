# Refine Writing

## Scope

Use when the researcher supplies academic-paper examples or instructions and
asks to learn a writing convention. It receives the examples, intended paper
context, and requested convention; it produces a concise, evidence-linked
addition to the shared writing guidance. Ask for clarification when the example
set, context, or proposed rule is ambiguous.
Primary agent role: writing-agent.

## Workflow

1. Read `docs/scholarly-writing-standard.md` and inspect only the supplied
   examples or instructions.
2. Identify a repeated, useful convention and its scope. Separate observed
   pattern from the researcher's preferred rule.
3. Propose a concise addition, examples of appropriate use, and exceptions or
   limits. Obtain confirmation before making it durable.
4. Update the shared guideline with source context and notify the applicable
   paper-development skills through the framework catalogue.

## Constraints

- Do not copy long passages, style-imitate a living author, or infer a universal
  standard from one example.
- Do not rewrite a paper or alter topic knowledge in this workflow.
