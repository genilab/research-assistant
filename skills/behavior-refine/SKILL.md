# Refine Behavior

## Scope

Use when the researcher supplies examples or instructions for a new research
practice. It receives the examples, intended use, and proposed behavior; it
produces a confirmed, bounded addition to a guideline or a proposal for a new
skill. Ask for clarification when the behavior, evidence, scope, or owner is
ambiguous.
Primary agent role: research-agent.

## Workflow

1. Inspect only the supplied examples and relevant current guideline or skill.
2. Separate observed pattern, researcher preference, activation condition,
   expected outcome, limits, and evidence for reuse.
3. Propose the smallest durable change: a guideline addition, skill revision, or
   new skill plan. Obtain researcher confirmation before changing it.
4. Route a new or revised skill through `skill-create` and refresh the framework
   catalogue after confirmation.

## Constraints

- Do not infer a universal behavior from one example or copy its substantive
  content into a general guideline.
- Do not silently change an existing workflow, agent authority, or topic record.

## Dependencies

- `skill-create`
