# Experimentation Guidelines

Every experiment begins with a research question, claim or hypothesis, and a
decision that its result should inform. The plan identifies design, comparison
or baseline, data or materials, variables or measures, procedure, analysis
method, validity threats, ethical or operational constraints, and stop or
revision conditions.

Catalog results with their experiment identifier, provenance, conditions,
measures, analysis, and interpretation. Keep observed results separate from
inferences. A result does not confirm a broader claim unless the design and
analysis support that scope.

Experiment reports state the question, design, materials, results,
interpretation, limitations, and implications. Link the report to its plan and
catalogued results through the topic experiment index and publication index.
