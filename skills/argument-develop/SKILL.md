# Develop Argument

## Scope

Use when the researcher says `Develop argument for {topic}` or asks to refine a
research question, contribution, claim, or scientific narrative. It receives an
existing topic, focus, and available evidence; it produces a linked argument
record. Ask for clarification when the question, intended claim, or evaluation
criteria are unclear.
Primary agent role: argument-agent.

## Workflow

1. Read the topic information, ideas, arguments, directions, relevant paper
   records, and references.
2. Define the question, provisional claim, audience, burden of proof, and
   material alternatives or counterarguments.
3. Link premises to evidence records; label inference, value judgment, and
   unsupported dependency separately.
4. Append the argument map to the `Arguments and directions` section of
   `{topic-slug}_ideas.md`, update topic `INFO.md`, and state next evidence or
   experiment needs.
5. Hand off paper structure to `structure-paper` or testable work to
   `experiment-plan` when appropriate.

## Constraints

- Do not present an argument as verified knowledge or manufacture supporting
  evidence.
- Do not draft a full paper or decide a contested thesis for the researcher.

## Dependencies

- `experiment-plan`
- `paper-propose`
