# Search Literature

## Scope

Use when the researcher says `Search literature for {topic}` and provides a
question, source constraints, or depth. It receives an existing topic and
search brief, then produces a traceable candidate-source list and selection
record. Ask for clarification when the question, allowed sources, date scope,
or intended depth is missing.
Primary agent role: literature-agent.

## Workflow

1. Read the topic question, existing references, and search brief; identify
   inclusion, exclusion, and duplicate-handling rules.
2. Search the approved academic sources, prioritizing primary papers and
   recording query, source, date, identifiers, and selection rationale.
3. Present candidate papers with source links and limitations. Do not imply that
   ranking, citation count, or a search result establishes quality.
4. Record the search log in the topic ideas record and route selected papers to
   `paper-learn` only after researcher confirmation.

## Constraints

- Do not claim systematic-review coverage without a documented search and
  selection method.
- Do not archive, analyze, or classify a paper without a separate request.

## Dependencies

- `paper-learn`
