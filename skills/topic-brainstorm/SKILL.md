# Brainstorm Topics

## Scope

Use when the researcher says `Brainstorm about {topic}`. It receives an
existing topic, its accumulated records, and a bounded prompt; it produces
research ideas, questions, hypotheses, arguments, directions, and next actions.
Ask for clarification when the topic or brainstorming focus is absent.
Primary agent role: knowledge-agent.

## Workflow

1. Read the topic INFO, discussion, information, ideas, arguments, directions,
   and relevant references.
2. Identify the bounded question, motivation, constraints, and decision the
   brainstorming should support.
3. Generate and compare ideas, hypotheses, questions, argument paths, and
   research opportunities. Distinguish supplied evidence from proposals.
4. Append durable outcomes to the appropriate sections of the ideas record;
   refresh topic INFO and recommend next exploration steps.

## Constraints

- Do not create a topic, claim a literature gap, or invent evidence.
- Do not turn a broad brainstorming request into an unbounded research search.
