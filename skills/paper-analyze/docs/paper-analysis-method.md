# Paper Analysis and Review Method

This method adapts the approval-gated process inspected in `FAU/Reviews` for
topic-local research records. It keeps source material intact and makes the
difference between extraction, analysis, classification, and peer review clear.

## Required analysis record

For an ingested paper, record its title, authors, source identity, central
thesis, key claims, line of reasoning, research domains or conceptual framing,
theoretical contribution, evidence basis, and limitations. Extract the complete
reference list separately and preserve each bibliographic entry verbatim.

## Review boundary

An analytical review describes what the paper claims, supports, and leaves
unresolved. A peer-review recommendation, score, or reviewer report is opt-in:
create it only when the researcher explicitly asks for one. Do not infer a
peer-review request from paper ingestion or analysis.

## Approval and retention

Confirm the target topic and source identity before archiving a supplied PDF.
Keep the original file unchanged. Maintain provisional extraction in the active
session until the researcher approves durable storage, then record only the
approved artifacts in the topic's `papers/` folder.
